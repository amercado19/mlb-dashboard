Park & spray fit

Citizens Bank Park plays ~8% above MLB average for home runs, with short-ish lines (LF ~329, RF ~330) that reward pull power. The field is 5 lefties to 3 righties, and the lefty pull crowd (Schwarber, Rice, Harper, Caglianone, Murakami) all aim at the short right-field porch. Tonight's warm 78° air adds carry; the light 6 mph south wind is a minor, mostly-neutral factor — not enough to reshape the bracket.

Reality check: the Derby is genuinely high-variance. It's batting-practice pitching (no live-pitch matchup), single-elimination brackets, and outcomes swing on timing, pace, stamina, and who's grooving that night — factors no model captures. Treat every projection here as a lean, not a lock, and size bets accordingly.
