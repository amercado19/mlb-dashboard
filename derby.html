Father-son Derby lore

The Guerreros lead it — Vlad Sr. won 2007, Vlad Jr. won 2023 (only father-son pair to both win; Jr. holds the single-Derby record, 91 in 2019). The Fielders (Prince won twice) and 3-time champ Ken Griffey Jr. round it out.

This year's thread: Ben Rice's dad, Dan Rice — a former Brown pitcher — is throwing to him. A real rhythm edge in a format that lives on timing.

Reality check: the Derby is high-variance — grooved BP, timing, pace and stamina swing it in ways no model nails, and the last-swing bonus adds more. Everything here is a lean; size bets small.
