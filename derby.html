Because you only get 20 cuts, the edge isn't season power totals — it's HR-per-swing conversion, loft, and stamina. A grooved, high-loft, repeatable swing beats raw exit velo that comes on line drives.

Who could win — the read

Favorite
Kyle Schwarber (L)

Most 400+ fly balls in the field (19), elite loft (30°+), the most repeatable high-volume swing, and it's his home park. The swing format rewards exactly his profile: groove it and rack up conversions. Chalk for a reason.

Live value
Junior Caminero (R) · Ben Rice (L)

Caminero — youngest legs (stamina matters more with the bonus rule), hottest bat, longest ball in the field (463 ft). Rice — the sleeper edge: his dad is throwing to him (nobody grooves him better), a lefty pull swing into the short RF porch, and high fly-ball volume. Both underpriced vs Schwarber.

Loft upgrade under the new format
Munetaka Murakami (L)

His season "expected HR" ranked last on volume, but the Derby doesn't care about volume — it cares about repeatable loft, and Murakami has the best launch in the field (32.2°) plus 99th-percentile barrels. On grooved BP that converts. More live than the odds suggest — a real dark horse.

Fade at short prices
Jac Caglianone (L) · Willson Contreras (R) · Jordan Walker (R) · Bryce Harper (L)

Caglianone hits it hardest (107.7 EV) but on the lowest loft (27.6°) — line-drive power plays worse in a Derby, boom-or-bust. Contreras/Walker have thump but middling conversion. Harper is the sentimental home pick and 2018 champ, but his projection is mid-pack — story over math.

Father-son Derby lore

The Guerreros are the gold standard — Vlad Sr. won in 2007, Vlad Jr. won in 2023, the only father-son pair to both take the crown (Jr. also holds the single-Derby record, 91 HRs in 2019). The Fielders (Prince won it twice) and three-time champ Ken Griffey Jr. round out the power bloodlines.

This year's father-son thread: Ben Rice's dad, Dan Rice — a former Brown University pitcher — is the one throwing to him tonight. It's a genuine comfort edge in a format that lives and dies on rhythm.

Reality check: even with the right format, the Derby is high-variance — grooved BP, timing, pace, and stamina swing outcomes in ways no model nails. The last-swing bonus adds even more randomness (a hot streak can blow past any projection). Treat all of this as leans, and size bets small.
