Father-son Derby lore

The Guerreros lead it — Vlad Sr. won 2007, Vlad Jr. won 2023 (only father-son pair to both win; Jr. holds the single-Derby record, 91 in 2019). The Fielders (Prince won twice) and 3-time champ Ken Griffey Jr. round it out.

This year's thread: Ben Rice's dad, Dan Rice — a former Brown pitcher — is throwing to him. A real rhythm edge, and the reason he grades out as our top longshot.

Reality check: this reweight deliberately fades the favorite, which is where value lives — but it's higher variance. It won't hit as often as chalk; it pays more when it does. Everything here is a lean — bet small, spread across the markets above, and enjoy the show.
